# iC+P website — admin guide

Everything on the website that changes over time is managed from the admin
panel. No technical knowledge is needed; if something goes wrong, nothing is
lost — just close the page and try again.

**Address:** `https://www.icp.firm.in/admin`
**Login:** your email and the password you were given. (No "sign up" — new
logins are created by the developer on request.)

Works on a laptop, desktop or tablet. It also opens on a phone if you need to
publish something on the move.

---

## The sidebar

| Section | What it manages |
|---|---|
| **Leads** | Design requests and general enquiries sent from the website form |
| **Recruitment** | Job applications sent from the website form |
| **Projects** | The portfolio — every project, its photos and details |
| **Team** | The people on the About Us page |
| **Clients** | The logos on the Clients page |
| **Testimonials** | Client quotes for the Testimonials page |
| **Site text & contact** | The About Us write-up, category introductions, and the phone / email / address / social links on the Enquiry page |

An orange number next to **Leads** or **Recruitment** is how many are still marked *New*.

## Adding a project (about a minute)

1. **Projects → + New project**
2. Fill in the **Project name**, choose the **Category**, add **Location** and
   **Area** (optional) and the **Description** (leave a blank line between paragraphs).
3. **Gallery photos:** drag photos into the box, or click it to choose. Several
   at once is fine, and phone photos are fine — large photos are shrunk and
   optimised automatically.
   - Drag a photo to change its order. The first one is shown first on the website.
   - **Use as thumbnail** makes that photo the one shown in the project list.
     If you don't choose, the first photo is used.
   - **Remove** takes a photo out (it is deleted when you save).
4. **Link name** is filled in automatically from the project name — this is the
   web address of the project (`…/portfolio/hospitality/hotel-hilton`). You
   rarely need to change it.
5. Switch **Published** on if it should be visible on the website now, or leave
   it as a **Draft** to finish later.
6. **Save.** You'll be taken back to the list; a published project is live
   immediately.

**Editing:** click the project's name in the list. **Reordering:** the ↑ ↓
arrows change the order within a category. **Hiding without deleting:** switch
the toggle in the list to Draft. **Deleting:** removes the project and all its
photos — the website asks you to confirm.

## Enquiries (Leads and Recruitment)

Every form submission is emailed to the studio *and* listed here. Click a row
to see the full message. From there you can:

- change its **Status** — *New* → *Contacted* → *Closed* (only New & Contacted
  are shown by default; use the filter to see Closed ones);
- add **Internal notes** for your own record (visitors never see these);
- **Reply by email** — opens your email program with the address filled in;
- **Delete** — removes it from the admin only; the Google Sheet keeps its copy.

The same enquiries also appear in the two Google Sheets in the studio's Drive,
**Website Leads** and **Website Recruitment**.

## Team, Clients, Testimonials

Each works the same way: **+ Add**, fill in the form, **Save**. Use **Edit** to
change an entry, ↑ ↓ to reorder, and the **Shown / Hidden** switch to take
something off the website without deleting it.

- **Team:** the *Write-up* appears in the panel that slides out when a visitor
  hovers the photo. *Role* is one line per row (e.g. `Founder` / `&` / `Design
  Director`), shown under the photo exactly as typed.
- **Clients:** upload a logo (PNG or SVG with a transparent background looks
  best — it is shown on brown), or choose *Text* for private clients without a
  logo.

## Site text & contact

- **About Us write-up:** one box, written like a document — a blank line
  starts a new paragraph; a line in CAPITALS on its own becomes a heading;
  start lines with `- ` for a bullet list (or `1. ` for numbers), and write
  `TITLE — text` in a list line to make the title bold; wrap a paragraph in
  quotes for a highlighted quote.
- **Category introductions:** the short text above the projects on each
  portfolio category page.
- **Contact details:** phone, email, address and the Instagram / LinkedIn /
  Pinterest links on the Enquiry page.

## Good to know

- Photos: JPG, PNG or HEIC. Landscape photos suit the project page; portrait
  photos suit thumbnails. Anything up to ~15 MB is fine.
- Changes are live the moment you save — there is no separate "publish website" step.
- If a page looks stale in your browser, refresh it.
- Forgotten password: ask the developer to reset it.
- The website deliberately shows a "please view on a tablet or desktop" screen
  on phones; the admin still works on phones.
